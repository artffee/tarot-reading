Major Arcana · IX

The Hermit

solitude · discernment · guidance

“A quiet light is enough for the next step.”

Upright

Step away from the chorus long enough to hear your own thinking. A modest light can reveal the next step without illuminating the entire road.

Reversed

Solitude may be turning into isolation, or constant company may be preventing reflection. Seek a rhythm of retreat and return.

Within the image

The star lantern illuminates a small circle in the cathedral's shadow, making inner guidance useful one step at a time.

A question to carry

What becomes clearer when you give yourself unhurried time?
