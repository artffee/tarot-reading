Minor Arcana · Seven · Pentacles

Seven of Pentacles

patience · assessment · cultivation

“I can be patient and still evaluate honestly.”

Upright

Review what your steady effort is producing. Some growth needs more time; some needs a changed method or a wiser use of energy.

Reversed

Impatience or sunk effort may be obscuring the evidence. Assess results honestly before committing another season.

Within the image

Pentacles growing among tended vines link practical reward with seasons, care, and the uncertainty of cultivation.

A question to carry

What evidence would tell you whether to persist or adjust?
