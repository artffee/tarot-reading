Three of Pentacles

craft · collaboration · learning

“I contribute my skill and welcome the skill of others.”

Upright

Good work grows when different skills meet with respect. Share the plan, invite feedback, and let each contribution be visible.

Reversed

Unclear roles or unrecognized effort may be undermining the work. Agree on standards and make room to learn from one another.

Within the image

The cathedral's three pentacles honor the craft and cooperation required to raise something larger than one person's effort.

A question to carry

What conversation would improve the way you work together?
