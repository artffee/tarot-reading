Major Arcana · XVIII

The Moon

uncertainty · imagination · instinct

“I can hold uncertainty without inventing certainty.”

Upright

Travel carefully through partial light. Notice dreams and feelings, then check assumptions before turning them into conclusions.

Reversed

Confusion may be clearing, or an unexamined fear may still be steering you. Separate what you know from what you imagine.

Within the image

The cathedral path under the moon holds ambiguity, reminding the traveler that a compelling shadow is not always a reliable map.

A question to carry

Which fear needs evidence, and which feeling needs care?
