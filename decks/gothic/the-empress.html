The Empress

nurture · creativity · abundance

“I make room for life to flourish.”

Upright

Care is a creative practice. Feed the project, relationship, or body that needs patient attention and let growth follow its own season.

Reversed

Overgiving or neglect may be draining the ground you depend on. Restore nourishment and boundaries before asking for more growth.

Within the image

The abbey garden brings living abundance into old stone, showing how tenderness can reclaim a place shaped by loss.

A question to carry

What would nourishment look like in one practical choice today?
