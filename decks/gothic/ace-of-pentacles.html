Ace of Pentacles

opportunity · grounding · resources

“I give possibility a practical beginning.”

Upright

A practical opportunity can become something lasting through patient care. Begin with a tangible step and a realistic sense of resources.

Reversed

A promising opening may need more preparation or a clearer commitment. Check the foundation before investing further.

Within the image

The golden pentacle above a stone hand brings possibility into the material world: time, shelter, work, and care.

A question to carry
