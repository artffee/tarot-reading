Knight of Wands

adventure · boldness · momentum

“I give my boldness a direction it can sustain.”

Upright

Bring courage to a meaningful pursuit. Enthusiasm is most useful when paired with enough follow-through to carry the work home.

Reversed

Impatience or changing direction too often may leave others carrying the consequences. Slow the charge and make a sustainable promise.

Within the image

The moving rider carries the suit's fire out of the sanctuary and into uncertain country.

A question to carry

What commitment would help your enthusiasm last?
