Minor Arcana · Four · Cups

Four of Cups

contemplation · apathy · receptivity

“I can pause and remain open to a new invitation.”

Upright

A pause may be asking you to understand what no longer nourishes. Look at the available offer without forcing yourself to want it.

Reversed

Interest may be returning after withdrawal, or avoidance may still be disguising itself as reflection. Test one gentle re-engagement.

Within the image

The still arrangement of cups holds both emotional weariness and the possibility of an unnoticed invitation.

A question to carry

What offer have you dismissed before understanding it?
