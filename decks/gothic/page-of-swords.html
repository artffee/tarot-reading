Page of Swords

inquiry · alertness · learning

“I can ask clearly and remain open to learning.”

Upright

Ask a precise question and follow the evidence with curiosity. Careful observation is more useful than a quick display of certainty.

Reversed

Rumor, defensiveness, or constant monitoring may be clouding attention. Verify what you repeat and let curiosity soften your stance.

Within the image

The young sword bearer watches the changing air, representing a mind learning how to use its edge.

A question to carry

Which assumption deserves a sincere question?
