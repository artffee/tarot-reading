Six of Cups

memory · tenderness · roots

“I can carry kindness forward without living in the past.”

Upright

A memory or familiar kindness may reconnect you with something simple and true. Receive its gift while remaining present to who you are now.

Reversed

Nostalgia may be smoothing away important truths or keeping you in an old role. Keep the tenderness and update the story.

Within the image

The cups carry the gentleness of an earlier season into the cathedral's weathered space.

A question to carry

Which remembered kindness could you offer in a new form?
