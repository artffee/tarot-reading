Eight of Pentacles

practice · skill · devotion

“My craft grows through attentive practice.”

Upright

Give repeated attention to a skill that matters. Improvement comes from useful feedback and the care you bring to ordinary work.

Reversed

Perfectionism or repetition without learning may be limiting progress. Revisit the purpose and practice the part that actually needs attention.

Within the image

The craftsperson's worked pentacles make learning visible through successive acts of care.

A question to carry

Which small part of the work deserves deliberate practice?
