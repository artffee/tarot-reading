Page of Cups

sensitivity · imagination · surprise

“I can meet my feelings with curiosity.”

Upright

An unexpected feeling or creative message deserves a curious welcome. Stay receptive without surrendering your judgment.

Reversed

Shyness, unrealistic expectations, or fear of embarrassment may be blocking expression. Offer a small honest message rather than a flawless one.

Within the image

The young cup bearer listens for a surprising voice within the vessel, giving imagination a playful form.

A question to carry

What small emotional truth could you express gently?
