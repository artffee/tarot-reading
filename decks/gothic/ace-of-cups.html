Minor Arcana · Ace · Cups

Ace of Cups

openness · feeling · renewal

“I make space to receive what is gentle.”

Upright

Let a fresh feeling arrive without immediately defining its future. A small gesture of kindness can begin a new emotional season.

Reversed

An empty cup or guarded heart may need attention before it can offer more. Receive care and allow feeling its own pace.

Within the image

The solitary cup offers water within the stone sanctuary, a living source in a place of quiet reflection.

A question to carry

What feeling could you welcome without needing to explain it?
