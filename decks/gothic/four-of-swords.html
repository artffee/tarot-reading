Four of Swords

rest · retreat · integration

“I give my mind permission to rest.”

Upright

Step back from the struggle long enough to recover perspective. Protected rest is part of the work, not a reward for finishing everything.

Reversed

Restlessness or depletion may be asking for a different pace. Return gradually and protect the recovery you have begun.

Within the image

The quiet chapel and resting swords make a sanctuary from constant reaction.

A question to carry

What can remain unanswered while you recover?
