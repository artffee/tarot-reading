Four of Pentacles

security · stewardship · control

“I can care for my resources without closing my life.”

Upright

Protect what you have built while asking what enough means. Good stewardship keeps resources useful rather than merely locked away.

Reversed

Fear of scarcity or careless release may be directing your choices. Find a boundary between hoarding and overgiving.

Within the image

Four pentacles create a guarded enclosure around the figure, making security and restriction visible together.

A question to carry

What would help security feel steady rather than fearful?
