Seven of Cups

imagination · options · discernment

“I can dream freely and choose carefully.”

Upright

Several possibilities are calling at once. Enjoy imagining, then look closely at what each option asks of your real life.

Reversed

Overwhelm or a seductive fantasy may be clearing. Choose one possibility to investigate rather than treating every image as a promise.

Within the image

The many cups hold differing visions, turning desire into a gallery that requires discernment.

A question to carry

Which possibility deserves facts before it earns commitment?
