Ten of Pentacles

legacy · continuity · shared-resources

“I help build a future others can inhabit.”

Upright

Consider the long life of your choices. A durable legacy includes knowledge, care, and fair access to what a household or community builds.

Reversed

Inherited expectations or unequal access may need examination. Strengthen the future by making belonging and responsibility more explicit.

Within the image

Pentacles arranged around the Gothic household connect personal effort to generations and a wider circle of care.

A question to carry

What do you want your work to make possible beyond yourself?
