Queen of Cups

empathy · intuition · care

“My care includes my own inner life.”

Upright

Listen deeply while staying rooted in your own emotional life. Compassion can hold another's feeling without taking ownership of it.

Reversed

Absorbing others' needs or withholding your own may be exhausting you. Restore a boundary that makes real care possible.

Within the image

The queen's attentive relationship with the cup gives feeling a place of dignity and reflective depth.

A question to carry

Where would a compassionate boundary help you listen more freely?
