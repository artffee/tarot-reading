King of Pentacles

stewardship · stability · mastery

“I use stability to support a wider good.”

Upright

Use experience and resources to create dependable conditions. Success becomes meaningful when it supports people and remains accountable.

Reversed

Status, possessiveness, or inflexibility may be replacing good stewardship. Reconnect material choices with the wellbeing they should serve.

Within the image

The pentacle sovereign's grounded throne represents responsibility for resources, continuity, and the lives supported by them.

A question to carry

How can what you have built become more useful and generous?
