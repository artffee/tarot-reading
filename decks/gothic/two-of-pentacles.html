Two of Pentacles

adaptability · priorities · rhythm

“I can adjust without abandoning what matters.”

Upright

Balance changing demands through deliberate adjustments. Protect your essentials and let the plan move rather than demanding a perfect equilibrium.

Reversed

Too many competing obligations may be overwhelming the rhythm. Choose what matters most and make the tradeoff explicit.

Within the image

Two gold pentacles held in motion show balance as a practiced rhythm rather than a motionless state.
