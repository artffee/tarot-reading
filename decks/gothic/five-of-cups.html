Minor Arcana · Five · Cups

Five of Cups

grief · perspective · remaining-gifts

“I honor what is lost and what remains.”

Upright

Let disappointment have its truthful weight. When you are ready, notice what remains and who can accompany you through the loss.

Reversed

Recovery may be beginning, though loyalty to sorrow can make relief feel disloyal. Let memory and renewed connection coexist.

Within the image

Fallen and standing cups share the same scene, giving loss and continuing possibility equal reality.

A question to carry

What still supports you beside this disappointment?
