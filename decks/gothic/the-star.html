Major Arcana · XVII

The Star

hope · renewal · openness

“I make space for hope to return.”

Upright

Let recovery be simple and sincere. A small act of care can restore trust in the future without requiring certainty.

Reversed

Hope may feel distant after strain. Lower the demand for inspiration and begin with a tangible source of replenishment.

Within the image

Starlight over the abbey offers orientation after darkness; the open night invites vulnerability without spectacle.

A question to carry

What modest source of renewal can you reach today?
