Minor Arcana · Three · Cups

Three of Cups

friendship · celebration · support

“I let friendship be a source of nourishment.”

Upright

Joy becomes richer when it is shared. Let trusted companions celebrate with you and offer the kind of belonging you can also give.

Reversed

Social strain, exclusion, or too many obligations may be obscuring real friendship. Seek a smaller, more nourishing circle.

Within the image

Three cups form a gathering within the Gothic world, making companionship a shelter rather than a performance.

A question to carry

Who could share a small joy or honest conversation with you?
