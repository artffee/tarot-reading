Major Arcana · XII

The Hanged Man

perspective · pause · surrender

“I allow a different view to emerge.”

Upright

A willing pause can reveal a view effort could not reach. Let an old assumption loosen before deciding what comes next.

Reversed

Waiting may have become a way to avoid a necessary choice. Distinguish a fruitful pause from a sacrifice that no longer serves you.

Within the image

The suspended figure changes the chapel's ordinary orientation, making stillness an active experiment in perspective.

A question to carry

What might change if you stopped pushing for a moment?
