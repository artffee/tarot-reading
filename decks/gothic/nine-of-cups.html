Minor Arcana · Nine · Cups

Nine of Cups

contentment · gratitude · pleasure

“I can savor enough.”

Upright

Enjoy a wish or comfort that has taken real shape. Gratitude becomes fuller when you let yourself experience satisfaction without hoarding it.

Reversed

A polished picture of success may be hiding an unmet need. Ask which pleasures nourish you and which only distract.

Within the image

The gathered cups create a chamber of plenty, inviting reflection on what makes abundance satisfying.

A question to carry

What is already good enough to enjoy without improving it?
